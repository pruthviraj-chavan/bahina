About the Division

Research that is rooted in India, rigorous in method, and relevant to people's lives

Bahina Research was established on the conviction that evidence should inform compassion, and that the communities most affected by policy deserve to have research conducted with them, not merely about them. Our approach is participatory, interdisciplinary and deeply contextual.


We work alongside universities, civil society organisations, government departments and international research bodies to produce findings that are accessible, actionable and ethically grounded. Our research spans ecology, social policy, gender studies, economic development and living heritage — the full breadth of what makes India's communities thrive or struggle.

Research Themes

Four integrated lenses on India's social and ecological reality

01

Ecology & Environmental Governance

Investigating forest commons, biodiversity corridors, coastal ecosystems, watershed dynamics and community-led conservation across India's most ecologically significant regions.

02

Rural Livelihoods & Economic Development

Field-based research on smallholder agriculture, informal economies, migration, women's enterprise and the political economy of rural transformation in South Asia.

03

Gender, Rights & Social Equity

Longitudinal studies on gender-based discrimination, land rights, access to public goods, political participation and the lived experience of marginalised communities.

04

Heritage, Culture & Living Traditions

Documenting and analysing India's intangible cultural heritage, traditional knowledge systems, craft economies and the intersection of tourism, identity and conservation.

Our Approach

How we work

🤝

Participatory Methods

Our research is designed and conducted alongside the communities it serves, ensuring that local knowledge and lived experience shape the research questions and findings.

🔬

Interdisciplinary Rigour

We bring together ecologists, economists, anthropologists, public health researchers and policy experts to address complex, multi-dimensional challenges.

📡

Open Access Policy

All Bahina Research publications are freely available. We believe knowledge generated in the public interest must be accessible to all — especially those affected by our findings.
