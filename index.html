The Foundation

A force for change across communities, knowledge and culture

Bahina Foundation is a multi-disciplinary Indian foundation working at the intersection of social impact, rigorous research and transformative hospitality. We believe that lasting change happens when people are empowered, knowledge is shared and places tell stories worth telling.

Founded with the belief that India's greatest resource is the strength of its communities, Bahina brings together three deeply integrated divisions — each reinforcing the other — to create a model of holistic, sustainable impact.

Our Three Divisions

Transforming lives through integrated action

Three divisions. One mission. Each of our programmes is designed to strengthen the others, creating a multiplier effect on our collective impact.

01 · NGO / Social Impact

Creating meaningful change, one community at a time

Our NGO division works at the grassroots to strengthen livelihoods, advance education, empower women and build resilient communities across rural India. We believe that sustainable development begins with people, not projects.

Learn about our impact
02 · Research

Generating knowledge for a more equitable future

The Bahina Research division produces rigorous, policy-relevant research across social development, ecology, economics and culture. Our findings inform both our own programmes and broader public discourse in India.

Explore our research
03 · Hospitality

Places that tell stories worth experiencing

Bahina Hospitality curates deeply rooted experiences — heritage stays, community immersions and cultural journeys that are themselves an act of preservation. Revenue from hospitality directly supports our social and research work.

Discover our spaces

From a mud-floor classroom to a scholarship — Priya's journey

In a small village in Vidarbha, Maharashtra, a girl who once walked four kilometres to reach school is now among the first from her community to pursue a degree in environmental science. Bahina's foundational learning programme gave Priya not just literacy, but the confidence to dream beyond the visible horizon.

Where We Work

Rooted in India, working across its most vital regions

Bahina Foundation currently operates across 14 Indian states, with deep community partnerships, research field stations and heritage hospitality properties. Our work is designed to be deeply contextual — understanding that every region of India has its own ecology, economy and culture.

We are present where the need is greatest and where authentic connections can be forged with local communities and ecosystems.

Featured Research

Understanding rural India's changing social and ecological fabric

Bahina Research produces policy-relevant, field-based research across social development, ecology, economics and cultural heritage. Our 2024–25 annual research programme spans four major themes: rural livelihoods, forest governance, heritage conservation and gender equity in South Asia.
