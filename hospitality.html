About the Division

Hospitality as preservation — spaces that breathe, properties that give back

Bahina Hospitality was born from an insight: that some of India's most extraordinary places — its havelis, village commons, forest lodges and coastal retreats — were quietly disappearing, and that responsible hospitality could be a force for their conservation.


Every Bahina property is deeply rooted in its ecology and culture. We restore, we conserve, we employ locally and we narrate honestly. A stay at a Bahina property is never just accommodation — it is a curated encounter with a living tradition, a landscape or a community. And crucially, every rupee of surplus from Bahina Hospitality flows directly to support the NGO and Research divisions of the Foundation.

Life at Bahina Properties

Our Mission

Travel that restores — the guest, the place and the community

We reject the model of extraction that has defined too much of Indian tourism. At Bahina, every property is a site of regeneration — of heritage, of ecology, of local culture and of local economy.

We work with local masons, weavers, farmers and artisans to create properties that are authentically of their place. We source food hyperlocally. We employ from the surrounding community. And we reinvest our surplus into the Foundation's social and research work.

Our Values

♻️

Regenerative Practice

From solar energy and rainwater harvesting to compost kitchens — every property is designed to give more back to its ecosystem than it takes.

🏘️

Community First

We employ locally, source locally and invest locally. A minimum of 80% of our operational staff come from the communities surrounding each property.

📖

Authentic Storytelling

Every Bahina property tells the story of its place honestly — its history, its struggles, its beauty and its people — without sanitisation or performance.
